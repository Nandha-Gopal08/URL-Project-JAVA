HTTPS & Reachability ≠ Safety

A URL being reachable or served over HTTPS only confirms that a server responded and the connection is encrypted.

  • HTTPS does not guarantee trust.
  • Reachable sites can contain malicious content.
  • Combine verification with reputation checks.
